Consumer Intelligence Layer

Trackly

by BatchTrack

The consumer-facing intelligence application. Scan Aztec codes, monitor expiry dates, verify warranties, review tax bills, and manage digital change credits.

  • 📦 Trackly Ownership: Categorized ledger for consumables, pharma, and electronics.
  • 🔍 Aztec Scanner: Instant mobile camera decoding linking directly to verified RAD dossiers.
  • ⏳ Expiry & Safety: Freshness prompt alerts and DCGI batch safety status indicators.
  • 💳 Change Credits: Personal wallet balance for unreturned change and store rewards.
  • 🎫 Service Requests: Raise verified warranty support tickets for tracked items.

Retailer Operating System

Avero

by BatchTrack

The B2B retailer operating system. Import batch spreadsheets, print Aztec & QR labels, run cashier POS stations, manage change credit debt, and handle support queues.

  • 📊 Inventory Upload: 8-column CSV and XLSX ingestion starting from row 2.
  • 🏷️ Dual-Code PDF: Automated printable sheets with side-by-side Aztec and QR labels.
  • 💻 Connected POS Stations: Multi-station cashier checkout with camera barcode scanning.
  • 🪙 Digital Change Credits: Eliminate loose change shortages by issuing credit notes.
  • 📋 RAD Service Queue: Inspect customer service claims with verified invoice data.
